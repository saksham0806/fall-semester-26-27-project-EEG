The mechanism

Gradient reversal — the whole idea in one layer

The domain head tries to name the recording corpus. The reversal layer sits between it and the backbone, and inverts the gradient on the way back — so the head gets better at identifying the device while the backbone is pushed to make that impossible.

Forward pass
y = x
Identity. The domain head sees the representation exactly as it is, so it learns to classify corpus honestly.
Backward pass
∂L/∂x ← −α · ∂L/∂x
Negated and scaled. The backbone receives the opposite of what would help the domain head, so it learns to discard corpus information.

α controls the strength. At α = 0 the reversal passes zero gradient and the adversary is switched off entirely — that row is the control, not a result.

  • The adversary barely worked. The best case removed 0.041 of corpus recoverability and cost 0.036 of accuracy — roughly a 1:1 trade, which buys nothing.
  • TSception went the wrong way. At full strength corpus became more recoverable (0.5938) while accuracy hit its worst (0.4521). Non-monotonic in α is the signature of training instability, not domain removal.
  • α is fixed, never ramped. Standard DANN anneals α from 0 → 1 so the adversary joins gradually; here it fights the task objective from the first step. That is the prime suspect and the first thing to change before concluding the method cannot work.
  • Do not compare 0.4763 to the 0.9161 feature-space probe. That figure is an SVM on 81 handcrafted features; this is a single dense head on a 128-d learned representation. Only each backbone's own α = 0 control is a valid reference.
Accuracy vs invariance

The trade-off is monotone

No model is both more invariant and more accurate — these five sit on a clean Pareto frontier. Corpus recoverability cannot fall below 0.4217, the rate you get by always guessing the largest corpus.

The verdict

This architecture did not achieve its goal. All 8 configurations missed the CogLoad within-corpus target, and the adversary never removed corpus information without taking an equal amount of task accuracy with it.

But the negative result is informative. Corpus-invariance and task accuracy moved together in every single configuration — which is exactly what you would predict once you accept that depression is a boolean identity with corpus == MODMA. Every depression segment is MODMA and every stress segment is not. A model that genuinely cannot tell the corpora apart also cannot tell depression from stress, because in this data they are the same distinction.

What would change the answer: α annealing rather than a fixed value, a stronger probe than a single dense head, and above all a corpus that contains more than two of the three classes. The first two are cheap; the third is data collection.
