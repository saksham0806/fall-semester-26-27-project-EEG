## The finding

**A mechanism check passes while the transfer it was supposed to underwrite fails.** Adversarial domain alignment demonstrably removes domain information from the representation — the pre-registered frozen-probe gate is met — and on the one held-out amplifier where the mechanism is operative it *erases* the transfer signal rather than protecting it (McNemar p = 0.0225 on the paired held-out subjects).

That dissociation is the result. It is a live methodological point about probe-based invariance evaluation in EEG: a probe can confirm that a mechanism does what it claims while telling you nothing about whether the mechanism helps. Either half alone would be weaker — the passing gate reads as success, the failed transfer reads as a null. Together they say the evaluation instrument and the objective come apart.

## Frozen domain probes (§8.1) and the gate (§8.2 → §6.5 item 4)

Targets: balanced chance 0.250, majority domain 0.4943.

**Gate: PASS by its pre-registered letter, and uninformative on effect size.** Criterion `anchor_probe(main) < anchor_probe(lambda0) - 0.05`, recorded in `results/preregistration.md` before measurement: 0.3605 against 0.3615, a margin of 0.0010. Only **2 of 5 folds** clear the threshold individually, and the per-fold margin is 0.0510 ± 0.0236 — the 0.05 bar sits on the mean. Pre-registration obliges reporting the verdict as specified; it does not oblige letting a 0.0010 margin carry narrative weight.

**The gate passes and the representation is not invariant.** The anchor probe remains +0.1105 above balanced chance and the full-set probe +0.0445 above the majority domain. Those two statements sit together because the gate tests *relative* removal against the method-off control, not invariance. A passing invariance-style gate on a still-separable representation is exactly the reading this report exists to prevent.

The adversary's own head accuracy is never a probe (§12 item 9). Silhouette (§8.2, superseded) could not adjudicate: all modes within ±0.004 of zero.

## Known confounds

- **Mumtaz is confounded with its acquisition setup** — EDF channel count predicts diagnosis at 0.8889 (baseline 0.5238). Full detail in `results/mumtaz_audit.md`. Quote no Mumtaz depression figure without it.
- **CogLoad carries ~8x EEGMAT's above-45 Hz power** after gating (29x before). No filter reaches it; see the rejection table.
- **Every CV number here is subject-disjoint but never device-disjoint.** The transfer rows are the only device-disjoint measurements.
