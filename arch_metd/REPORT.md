## The finding

**The mechanism works, and the outcome it was built to protect has no signal to protect.** Adversarial domain alignment measurably removes domain information from the representation — the pre-registered frozen-probe gate is met on *both* independent split draws. On the held-out amplifier it was meant to serve, a model trained on **shuffled labels transfers as well as either real arm** (permuted 20/36, lambda=0 21/36, lambda=1 18/36), so the stress transfer carries no signal in any condition.

That dissociation is the result, and it is not the one this project set out to find. A probe can confirm a mechanism does exactly what it claims while saying nothing about whether the task it serves is learnable at all. The probe alone would read as a working method; the transfer alone would read as a method failure. Neither is true — the method operates, on a target that is inert.

**An earlier version of this report claimed the adversary *destroyed* stress transfer** (McNemar p = 0.0225 on split A). It did not survive a second, equally valid split (p = 0.5811), and the permutation control — run for the first time alongside split B — shows why: there was no transfer signal to destroy. That claim is withdrawn. Two arbitrary draws plus a label-permutation null are what separated a finding from a fluctuation.

**What survives everything:** depression transfers across amplifiers, in both directions, on both draws, clear of its own permutation null (Mumtaz->MODMA 0.6638 / 0.6667 vs null 0.5431; MODMA->Mumtaz 0.8675 / 0.8438 vs null 0.3998) — with the montage confound in `results/mumtaz_audit.md` still attached to the second pair.

**Gate: PASS by its pre-registered letter, and uninformative on effect size.** Criterion `anchor_probe(main) < anchor_probe(lambda0) - 0.05`, recorded in `results/preregistration.md` before measurement: 0.4019 against 0.4113, a margin of 0.0094. Only **3 of 5 folds** clear the threshold individually, and the per-fold margin is 0.0594 ± 0.0356 — the 0.05 bar sits on the mean. Pre-registration obliges reporting the verdict as specified; it does not oblige letting a 0.0094 margin carry narrative weight.

**The gate passes and the representation is not invariant.** The anchor probe remains +0.1519 above balanced chance and the full-set probe +0.0632 above the majority domain. Those two statements sit together because the gate tests *relative* removal against the method-off control, not invariance. A passing invariance-style gate on a still-separable representation is exactly the reading this report exists to prevent.

The adversary's own head accuracy is never a probe (§12 item 9). Silhouette (§8.2, superseded) could not adjudicate: all modes within ±0.004 of zero.

### The adversary's effect on transfer, tested as a contrast

Both arms are scored on the **same 36 held-out EEGMAT subjects**, so the claim "the adversary changes transfer" is a paired question. McNemar's exact test on the 13 discordant pairs: **p = 0.5811** (8 subjects correct only without the adversary, 5 only with it).

Two separate one-sample binomials do not test that contrast and neither clears 0.05 alone (λ=1 p=1.000, λ=0 p=0.405).

**λ=1 scores 18/36 = 0.5000, which is not significantly below chance.** The defensible claim is that the adversary *erases* the transfer signal, not that it reverses it — "below chance" invites an anti-learning reading these data do not support.


⚠️ **For the single-corpus runs the λ=0 column is not a method control.** The adversary is off in both arms; λ=0 additionally removes the early-stopping lock, so it may select a much earlier checkpoint (`dep_modma_to_mumtaz`: best epoch 12 vs 0). Only the EEGMAT direction's λ comparison is a method comparison.


⚠️ **Three of the four directions train on a single corpus**, so the adversary has nothing to align against and is disabled at source. Only `stress_sam40cog_to_eegmat` can test domain adaptation at all.

## Known confounds

- **Mumtaz is confounded with its acquisition setup** — EDF channel count predicts diagnosis at 0.8889 (baseline 0.5238). Full detail in `results/mumtaz_audit.md`. Quote no Mumtaz depression figure without it.
- **CogLoad carries ~8x EEGMAT's above-45 Hz power** after gating (29x before). No filter reaches it; see the rejection table.
- **Every CV number here is subject-disjoint but never device-disjoint.** The transfer rows are the only device-disjoint measurements.
