The mechanism

Why routing beats a single model

A flat classifier learns one decision boundary for all three corpora at once. CogLoad is a third of the data and every flat model scores below its majority class there — cancelling the genuine gain MODMA provides. Splitting the problem lets each corpus keep what it can actually learn.

  • Router and specialists want opposite feature sets. v2's 273 features route at 0.9703 against v1's 0.9161 — more features make the corpus easier to identify. But those same 273 collapse the MODMA specialist to 0.5280, below its own majority: 273 dimensions against 53 subjects overfits. Splitting the two views was worth +0.033, the single largest gain.
  • The guard makes 0.6267 a floor. A specialist is used only if it beats its corpus majority in inner cross-validation on the training folds. Deciding that on the test fold would be selection on the test set.
  • Only MODMA's specialist fires. SAM40 and CogLoad never beat guessing, so the guard falls back to their majority class. The entire gain comes from depression detection.
  • Tuning did nothing (+0.0002 from a nested C/γ grid), and letting the model pick its own feature view cost −0.025 — inner CV over ~40 subjects cannot reliably choose, so selection thrashes per fold.

Stress is nearly always caught (recall 0.954) because two of three corpora default to it. The real weakness is healthy — 2,243 healthy windows are called stress, almost all of them SAM40 and CogLoad baseline recordings absorbed by those majority fallbacks.

Read this before quoting 0.6820

Stage 1 consumes corpus identity. So does the 0.6267 baseline it is measured against — but state it explicitly rather than letting a reader assume otherwise.

Report the corpus-free row, never the oracle row. The 0.6963 variant reads the true corpus label at inference and exists only to bound how much accuracy the router's 3% error costs.

This is not a deployment estimate. Every result here is subject-disjoint but never device-disjoint. Corpus identity is ~97% recoverable from the features, and two independent measurements — removing the device signature (0.3255) and applying a specialist to an unseen corpus (0.3995) — put accuracy on new hardware near chance.

The ceiling is structural. No corpus contains more than two of the three classes: every depression segment is MODMA and every stress segment is not. Stress versus depression is therefore a device distinction, not a neural one, and no architecture fixes that from this data.
